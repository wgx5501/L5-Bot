Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026
This Privacy Policy explains what information L5 Bot may store and why it is needed to provide its logging and protection features.

1. Guild Data

  • Guild ID
  • Channel and role IDs
  • Logging configurations
  • Protection, Anti-Raid, and Anti-Nuke settings
  • Whitelisted users, roles, and bots

2. User Data

L5 Bot may store User IDs and information related to moderation, logging, and security events when required to provide its features.

Message Content: L5 Bot does not intentionally store users' message content.

3. Why We Store Data

Data is used to operate logging and protection features, save server configurations, identify users involved in logged events, and maintain the reliability and security of L5 Bot.

4. Data Storage

Data is stored on the infrastructure used to host L5 Bot. Access is limited to authorized personnel responsible for operating and maintaining the service.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your data. Data is not shared with third parties except where necessary to operate the service, provide a requested feature, comply with legal requirements, or protect the security of L5 Bot and its users.

6. Data Retention & Deletion

Server configuration and security data may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service. You may contact the L5 Bot Support Team to request deletion of data associated with you or your server, subject to legitimate security, legal, or operational requirements.

7. Children's Privacy

L5 Bot does not knowingly collect personal information from children where such collection is prohibited by applicable law.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Changes become effective when the updated policy is published on this page.

9. Contact Us

For privacy questions, data requests, bug reports, or security concerns, please contact the L5 Bot Support Team through the official support server.
